Our Story

From a Small Unit
to a Global Name

Clover 7 was founded in 2008 in Tirupur — India's knitwear capital — with a single goal: to deliver honest quality to global brands. What started as a modest knitting unit with 40 workers has grown into a full-service, vertically integrated garment manufacturer trusted by buyers in Europe, North America, Australia and the Middle East.

Our strength lies in our people. Over 70% of our 500+ strong workforce are women from local communities in Tirupur and surrounding areas. We invest in their skills, safety and well-being — because we believe that great garments come from empowered people.

From yarn to finished garment — every process happens under our roof, giving us full control over quality, lead times and cost. Whether you need 2,000 pieces or 200,000, Clover 7 delivers on time, every time.
